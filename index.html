Turn any chapter into a bright, ready-to-teach PowerPoint in minutes. Review, edit, and upload to your ICT portal.

Setup check

    AI setup (free, one time)

    1. Open Google AI Studio and sign in with your Google account.
    2. Create an API key and copy it.
    3. Paste it below, press Save, then Test.

    The key is saved only in this browser on this device, so don’t use a shared computer. The free Gemini tier may use submitted content to improve Google’s products, so never enter student names, photos, or marks. If the model name stops working, update it from the list in AI Studio.

    1. Tell us about your lesson

    Slides are made for one chapter at a time. For best accuracy paste the chapter text or upload page photos. If you leave both empty, slides are drafted from the standard curriculum and may not match your textbook, so please review them. Searching for the textbook PDF automatically needs the hosted version.

    No PDF saved.

    While a PDF is saved, it is used for every chapter number you enter. If you upload page photos for a lesson, the photos are used instead.

    Please avoid photos showing student names, faces, or marks.

    Include
    Works best with a few paragraphs of text.

    2. Review and edit

    This is an AI-assisted draft. Please check facts, sums, and quiz answers before teaching.

    Please don’t enter student names, photos, or marks. Your lesson text is sent to Google’s Gemini service, using your own key, only to create the draft.
